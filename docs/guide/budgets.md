# Budgets

A budget sets what you expect each account to bring in or cost in each month of a fiscal year.
You can also budget the balance you expect on balance sheet accounts at each month end. Budgets
never change your books: they are only compared with them in reports.

## Create a budget

1. Go to **Accounting › Budgets** (or **Company › Planning & Budgeting › Set Up Budgets**).
2. Click **New budget…**.
3. Enter a **Name**. It starts as *FY2026 Budget* for the current year; each name can be used once.
4. Choose the **Fiscal year containing**: last year, this year or one of the next two. The budget
   covers the twelve months of the fiscal year that contains June 30 of that year. Your fiscal
   year starts in the **First month of fiscal year** preference (see [Choose your accounting
   settings](getting-started.md#choose-your-accounting-settings)). With a calendar year, 2026 means
   January to December 2026; with a year starting in July, 2026 means July 2025 to June 2026.
   The **Budget** list shows the start date, so check it after you create the budget.
5. Click **Create budget**.

You can keep several budgets for the same year, such as a plan and a stretch target. Choose which
one to work on in **Budget** at the top; each shows when its fiscal year starts.

A budget is by account and month. Budgets by class or by customer or job aren't available yet.

## Enter the amounts

1. Choose the **Budget**.
2. Set **Accounts** to **Profit and Loss** (the default) or **Balance Sheet**. Only active accounts
   are listed.
3. Type an amount in each month's box. The columns are the fiscal year's months, such as *Jan 2026*.
   - For income and expense accounts, enter what you expect for that month. **Total** adds up the
     row.
   - For balance sheet accounts, enter the **balance** you expect at the end of the month, not the
     change.
4. To use the same amount every month, type it in the first month and click **Copy across** at the
   end of the row. It replaces the other eleven months.
5. Click **Save**. The button shows how many boxes changed.

Amounts are entered in your home currency. An amount that isn't a number is outlined in red, and
**Save** stays off until you fix it. Clearing a box, or entering 0, removes that month's amount.

While there are unsaved changes, you can't switch to another budget: save them first.

## Delete a budget

Choose the budget and click **Delete**, then confirm. Its amounts are removed. Your transactions
aren't affected.

## Budget reports

- **Budget vs. Actual** at the top of **Accounting › Budgets** opens **Profit and Loss Budget vs.
  Actual**, or **Balance Sheet Budget vs. Actual** when **Accounts** is set to **Balance Sheet**.
  Both are explained in [Budget vs. Actual](reports.md#budget-vs-actual).
- **Reports › Budgets & Forecasts › Budget Overview** lists every budget for the fiscal year that
  contains the date you choose: each account's amount by month with a total, income first, then
  expenses, and the budgeted net income (income less expenses). Balance sheet amounts are listed
  but left out of net income.
- **Reports › Budgets & Forecasts › Budget vs. Actual Graph** draws actual against budget by
  month (see [Graphs](reports.md#graphs)).
