# Track time

Time entries record hours worked. They don't post to your books by themselves; billable hours can
be invoiced.

## Add an employee

In **Employees › Time Tracking**, type a name in **New employee name** and click **Add**.

## Enter a single activity

1. Choose **Single time activity** (or **+ New › Single Time Activity**).
2. Choose the **Employee** and **Date**, and enter **Time (hh:mm or 1.5)**, for example `1:30` or
   `1.5`.
3. To bill it, tick **Billable**, then choose the **Customer**, the **Service** and the
   **Rate / hr**. Billable time needs a customer and a rate.
4. Click **Save time**.

## Enter a weekly timesheet

1. Choose **Weekly timesheet** (or **+ New › Weekly Timesheet**).
2. Choose the **Employee** and **Week starting (Monday)**.
3. Enter the hours for each day. **Week total** adds them up.
4. Click **Save timesheet**.

Saved activities are listed under **Time activities**.
