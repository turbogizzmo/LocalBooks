# Reminders and the To Do list

**Reminders** sum up what needs attention today: overdue invoices, bills coming due, to-dos,
recurring transactions to enter, forms waiting to be emailed, federal tax dates and a backup that
is overdue. The **To Do list** holds your own tasks, with due dates, about a customer, vendor or
employee. Both stay inside LocalBooks: nothing is sent anywhere, and there are no system
notifications.

## See your reminders

The **Reminders** panel is on the right of the **Dashboard** (also **Company › Reminders**). Each
line is a category with the number of items in it, and a total for invoices and bills.

- Click a category to expand it and see its items. Click an invoice, bill or to-do with a posted
  transaction to open that transaction.
- The link at the end of each line opens the place to deal with it: **Invoices**, **Pay Bills**,
  **To Do List**, **Recurring Transactions**, **Send Forms**, the **Tax calendar** or **Back up**.
- Categories with nothing in them are hidden. When nothing needs attention, the panel says so.

| Reminder | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Overdue invoices | Posted invoices with an open balance, more than the chosen days past due (0 = any day late) |
| Bills to pay | Unpaid bills due within the chosen days, including overdue ones |
| To-dos due | Open to-dos due within the chosen days (0 = due today or earlier). With users on: yours and unassigned ones |
| Recurring transactions to enter | Active recurring transactions whose next date falls within the chosen days |
| Forms to email | Invoices, estimates and sales receipts marked **Email later** |
| Tax dates | Federal return and estimated-tax due dates for your tax form within the chosen days |
| Backup | Shown when the newest backup in the backups folder is older than the chosen days, or there is none |

QuickBooks also offers "Forms to print" and "Inventory to reorder". LocalBooks has no print queue
and no reorder points yet, so these reminders aren't available.

## Choose your reminders

1. In the Reminders panel, click **Customize**.
2. Tick the reminders to **Show** and set each one's **Days**: how far ahead to look, how late an
   invoice must be, or how old a backup may get.
3. Tick **Show Reminders when the company opens** to see them in a window each time you open the
   company (off at first). It shows once per company per run of the app, and not when you open a
   company without restoring windows.
4. Click **Save**.

With users on, these choices are each user's own, and every role can change them.

## Add a to-do

1. Choose **+ New › To Do**, click **New To Do** in **Accounting › To Do List**, or choose
   **New to-do…** in a customer's or vendor's ▾ menu (the to-do is then about them).
2. Enter what **To do**, and optionally a **Due date** and a **Note**.
3. Under **About**, choose a customer, vendor or employee. For a customer you can then link one of
   their **Invoices**; for a vendor, one of their **Bills**.
4. With users on, choose who it's **Assigned to** (or leave **Anyone**).
5. Click **Save**.

## Work through the To Do list

**Accounting › To Do List** (also **Company › To Do List**) lists to-dos with the earliest due
first; overdue dates are in red.

- Filter by **Not done**, **Done** or **All**; by due date (**Overdue**, **Due today or earlier**,
  **Due within 7 days**, **No due date**); with users on, by who it's assigned to; or find text in
  the title, note or name.
- Tick **Done** to complete a to-do, and untick it to reopen it.
- Double-click a row, or choose **Edit…** in its ▾ menu, to change it. The menu also opens the
  linked invoice or bill and deletes the to-do.

## Users and roles

- **Admin** and **Standard** users add, edit, complete and delete any to-do.
- **View only** users see the To Do list and their reminders, and can tick off only the to-dos
  assigned to them. They can still choose their own reminders.
