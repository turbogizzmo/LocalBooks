# Bank deposits and transfers

## Deposit payments from Undeposited Funds

When several customer payments go to the bank together, record one deposit so your books show the
same single amount as your bank statement.

1. Go to **Banking › Deposits & Transfers** and click **Bank deposit** (or **+ New › Bank
   Deposit**).
2. Choose **Deposit to** (the bank account) and the **Date**. **Ending balance** beside it shows
   the account's balance before this deposit.
3. Tick the payments waiting in Undeposited Funds that are part of this deposit.
4. To include other money (for example a refund), use **Add funds to this deposit** with an
   account and amount.
5. If you kept some of it as cash (for petty cash, say), fill in **Cash back goes to** (the account
   the cash goes to), a memo, and the **Cash back amount**. The bank gets the subtotal less the
   cash back; the cash back must be less than the subtotal.
6. Check **Total deposit** against your deposit slip, then click **Save & Post Deposit**.

## Type a deposit into the register

A deposit that isn't a customer payment (a refund, a loan, cash from the owner) can be typed
straight into the bank account's register: see
[Type a check or deposit into a register](expenses-and-checks.md#type-a-check-or-deposit-into-a-register).

## Move money between your accounts

1. Click **Transfer** (or **+ New › Transfer**).
2. Choose **Transfer funds from** and **Transfer funds to**, and enter the **Amount**, **Date** and an
   optional **Memo**.
3. Click **Save & Post Transfer**.

A transfer isn't income or an expense, so it doesn't appear on the Profit and Loss.

## Pay down a credit card

1. Choose **Banking › Pay Down Credit Card…**, or click **Pay down card** on this screen.
2. Choose the **Credit card**; **Amount** starts at what it owes. Choose the bank account to **Pay
   from**, and change the amount for a partial payment.
3. Click **Save & Pay**.

It posts as a transfer: the card's balance and the bank both go down. It appears in this list and
in both accounts' registers, ready to match on each statement when you reconcile.

To record a customer's check the bank returned, see
[Record a bounced check](customer-payments.md#record-a-bounced-check).

> **Tip:** Voiding a deposit returns its customer payments to Undeposited Funds.
