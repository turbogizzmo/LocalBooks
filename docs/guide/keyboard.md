# Use LocalBooks from the keyboard

Everything in LocalBooks can be done without a mouse: every page in the sidebar, every form and
dialog, the lists, the reports and Help. This page gathers the keys in one place. On a Mac, **Alt**
is the **Option** key and most **Ctrl** shortcuts are **⌘**.

LocalBooks aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA: every
field has a name a screen reader reads out, focus is always visible, and text has enough contrast
against its background. An automated check (axe) runs over every page in the sidebar, every form on
**+ New**, the **+ New** dialog and Help on Windows and Linux before each change is accepted.

## Move between the sidebar, the toolbar and the window

LocalBooks' window has three parts: the **sidebar** on the left, the **toolbar** across the top (the
page title, search, your sign-in, text size and Help) and the **window** you are working in.

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **F6** | Moves to the next part: sidebar, then toolbar, then the active window, then round again. |
| **Shift+F6** | Moves to the part before. |
| **Tab** / **Shift+Tab** | Moves through the fields and buttons of the part you are in. In a window, Tab goes round that window's own fields and buttons and doesn't leave it; press **F6** to get out. |

Coming back to a part with **F6** puts you on the field you left there. In the sidebar, F6 lands on
the page that is open.

On a Mac the function keys may need **fn** (**fn+F6**), unless **Use F1, F2, etc. keys as standard
function keys** is on in the Mac's keyboard settings.

## Open a page

1. Press **F6** until you are in the sidebar.
2. Press **Tab** or **Shift+Tab** to move to the page you want. A group heading (**Sales**,
   **Expenses**, …) folds its pages away or opens them again with **Enter** or **Space**.
3. Press **Enter**. The page opens in its window, and **F6** takes you to it.

To start a transaction from anywhere, press **Ctrl+Alt+N** (⌘⌥N on a Mac) for **+ New**: move with
the arrow keys, press **Enter** to open a form and **Esc** to close it. The classic shortcuts also
open screens directly (**Ctrl+I** for an invoice, **Ctrl+F** for Find Transactions, and the others
in [Find things in the menu bar](getting-started.md#find-things-in-the-menu-bar)).

## Work with windows

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Ctrl+F6** or **Ctrl+Tab** | Brings the next open window to the front (with **Shift**, the one before). |
| **Esc** or **Ctrl+F4** | Closes the active window. Esc is left to a menu, a dialog or a list of suggestions while one is open, and to a field you are typing in. |
| **Ctrl+Shift+N** | Opens another LocalBooks window (⇧⌘N on a Mac). |
| **Ctrl +** / **Ctrl −** / **Ctrl 0** | Larger text, smaller text, or back to normal, for this kind of window. |

See [Work in more than one window](getting-started.md#work-in-more-than-one-window) for the rest.

## Lists and menus

In a list, **Tab** reaches the first row, **↑** and **↓** move between rows, **Home** and **End**
go to the first and last, and **Enter** opens the row. The row's **▾** menu is one more Tab away:
**Enter** or **Space** opens it, the arrow keys choose and **Esc** closes it. See
[Move around a list from the keyboard](getting-started.md#move-around-a-list-from-the-keyboard)
and [Work without a mouse](getting-started.md#work-without-a-mouse).

## Forms

**Tab** moves through a form's fields in reading order. **Alt+S** saves and closes, **Alt+N** saves
and starts a new one, and date boxes take **t** for today and **+** and **−** for a day later or
earlier. Every key is in
[Save a form and type faster with the keyboard](getting-started.md#save-a-form-and-type-faster-with-the-keyboard).

## Menus and Help

- **F1** opens Help on the screen you are on, and closes it again.
- On Windows, press **Alt** to reach the menu bar (for example **Alt+U** for Customers). On a
  Mac, **Ctrl+F2** moves to the menu bar when keyboard navigation is on in the
  Mac's keyboard settings.
- Every menu command is listed in [The menu bar](menus.md).
